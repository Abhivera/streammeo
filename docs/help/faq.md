# FAQ

**Who is Streammeo?**  
Software that companies use to run support. Your merchant or provider answers you — not Streammeo.

**How fast will I get a reply?**  
Depends on the company's hours and policies. Many aim for a few hours on business days.

**I can't log in / reset my password**  
Contact the company whose product you use. They handle account access.

**Is my chat or email private?**  
Visible to that company's support agents. See their privacy policy for details.

**My link expired**  
Email support again or start a new chat. Mention your old ticket number if you have it.

**Are you a support agent?**  
See [Team documentation](../README.md) at `/docs`.
