# Rate your experience

After your issue is **resolved**, the company may email a short survey.

1. Open the link in the email.
2. Pick a score from **1** (poor) to **5** (excellent).
3. Add an optional comment.
4. Submit — you can only answer once per ticket.

Your feedback helps them improve support. If the link expired, you can ignore it — your ticket is still resolved.

For unresolved problems, reply on your ticket link or email support directly instead of only using the survey.
