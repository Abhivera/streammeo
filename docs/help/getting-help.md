# How to get help

When a company uses Streammeo, you can usually reach them by:

1. **Email** — their support address (e.g. support@company.com).
2. **Live chat** — the chat bubble on their website.
3. **Ticket link** — a secure link they emailed you to view and reply in your browser.

## What to include

- Your name and account email
- A clear description of the problem
- Order or invoice numbers if relevant
- Screenshots for billing or technical issues

**Never** send passwords or full card numbers. Legitimate support will not ask for your password.

## Who answers?

The **company you contacted** handles your request — not Streammeo. Streammeo is the software they use behind the scenes.
