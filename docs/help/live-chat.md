# Using live chat

## Start a chat

1. Find the chat icon (usually bottom-right).
2. Click to open the panel.
3. Type your question and send.

You may get an automatic reply first — that means your message was received.

## While you wait

- Reply times depend on the company's hours and workload.
- Say if the issue is **urgent** (locked out, double charge, etc.).
- Leave your email if asked, so they can follow up.
- When a support agent joins, you'll see a message like "**Name** has joined the chat" and can talk to them in real time.

## After the chat

Harder issues often become a **support ticket** with email updates. You may get a link to track progress — see [Track your request](./track-your-request.md).
